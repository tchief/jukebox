The Lakeside Science HallExhibit notes · Our Ocean

The ocean's warm conveyor belts

Currents carry heat from the tropics toward the poles. In this NASA visualization, colour shows the temperature of the sea surface while the currents swirl across the globe. Press Play sound to hear it: warmer water rises in pitch, the melody sweeps the map from west to east.

Visitors who cannot see the screen can press Describe for a spoken summary, or follow the music alone.

Add it to your page

<script type="module" src="https://YOUR-SITE/embed.js"></script>
<earth-orchestra svs="3912" sound="orchestra"></earth-orchestra>

Use any SVS visualization number in svs. Sounds: orchestra, raw, lullaby, evening-raga, space. The original visual is never changed and sound starts only when the visitor asks for it.
